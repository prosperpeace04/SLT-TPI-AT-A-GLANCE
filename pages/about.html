What is Science Laboratory Technology?

Science Laboratory Technology (SLT) is a two-year National Diploma programme offered by The Polytechnic Ibadan under the Faculty of Science. SLT students are trained in the practical and theoretical aspects of laboratory science — covering chemistry, biology, physics, electronics, microbiology, analytical chemistry, and more.

Graduates of SLT work in hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, government laboratories, food and beverage industries, and petrochemical companies. SLT is one of the most versatile science diplomas available at this level.

Courses Covered on This Platform

  • STC 211 — Inorganic Chemistry II (Group elements, water chemistry)
  • STC 212 — Analytical Chemistry (Spectrophotometry, AAS, Beer-Lambert Law)
  • STP 211 — Electronics (Semiconductors, diodes, transistors, valves)
  • Microbiology — Control of Microorganisms (sterilization, disinfection)
  • Computer Studies — Introduction to Microsoft Excel for SLT
  • Research Methodology and Basic Statistics
  • ND2 Report Books — Biochemistry (STC 222), Genetics (STB 221), Ecology (STB 222), Maintenance & Repair (STP 221), Glass Blowing (GLT 222)
