About the Programme

Science Laboratory Technology (SLT) is a National Diploma programme accredited by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE). At The Polytechnic Ibadan, the programme runs under the Faculty of Science and admits students through JAMB and direct entry routes.

The programme blends theoretical study with heavy laboratory practice. Students spend significant time in chemistry labs, biology labs, physics labs, and specialized SLT labs. The goal is to produce competent laboratory technologists who can work in any scientific setting.

The programme runs for two years (four semesters) leading to a National Diploma (ND). Strong CGPA graduates may proceed to Higher National Diploma (HND) or degree programmes in related fields.

SIWES

Students are required to undergo the Students' Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) — a period of industrial attachment in relevant industries and laboratories. SIWES is compulsory for the award of the National Diploma.

Attendance Requirement

Students must attend at least 70% of lectures and practical sessions per semester. Failure to meet this disqualifies a student from sitting exams in the affected course(s).

Admission Requirements

  • Five O'Level credits including Mathematics, English Language, Chemistry, Biology or Physics and any other Science subject.
  • Credits must be obtained in not more than two sittings.
  • JAMB score meeting The Polytechnic Ibadan's cut-off mark for SLT.
  • Pass Post-UTME screening (if applicable).

The Polytechnic Ibadan

The Polytechnic Ibadan was established in 1970. It is one of Nigeria's oldest polytechnics, located in Ibadan, the capital of Oyo State. The institution is governed by the NBTE and runs a wide range of ND and HND programmes.

Faculty of Science houses the Department of Science Laboratory Technology. The faculty is known for its practical, hands-on approach to science education.
